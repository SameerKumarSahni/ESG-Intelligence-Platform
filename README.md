# 📖 Overview

Organizations generate large amounts of sustainability-related information across reports, spreadsheets, operational records, and different departments.

Turning this information into meaningful ESG metrics and reports can be **manual, fragmented, and time-consuming**.

The **National ESG & Sustainability Intelligence Platform** provides a unified environment for collecting, analyzing, and understanding sustainability data.

The platform combines:

* 🌱 ESG analytics
* 📊 Carbon analytics
* 🤖 Generative AI
* 📄 AI-assisted report generation
* ⚖️ Compliance tracking
* 📈 Executive dashboards
* 💡 AI-powered recommendations

The goal is to move organizations from simply **reporting sustainability performance** to using sustainability data for **better decisions and continuous improvement**.


# 📊 ESG Scoring Methodology

The platform calculates an internal **Platform ESG Assessment Score**.

The assessment consists of:

```text
Environmental
Social
Governance
```

A configurable weighted model can be used:

```text
Overall ESG Score =
(Environmental × E Weight)
+
(Social × S Weight)
+
(Governance × G Weight)
```

Example configuration:

```text
Environmental = 40%
Social = 30%
Governance = 30%
```

The individual component scores are calculated from relevant organizational indicators.

The scoring methodology is intended for **decision support and demonstration purposes**.

It is not an official ESG certification or regulatory rating.

---

# 🌍 Carbon Analytics Methodology

Carbon analytics use configurable emission factors.

A simplified calculation can be:

```text
Estimated CO₂e =
Activity Data × Emission Factor
```

Examples:

```text
Electricity consumption × electricity emission factor
```

```text
Fuel consumption × fuel emission factor
```

The platform aggregates estimated emissions by:

* Month
* Year
* Facility
* Source

All carbon values should be interpreted as **estimates based on the configured assumptions and emission factors**.

# ⚠️ Limitations

This project is a **hackathon MVP/prototype**.

Potential limitations include:

* Carbon calculations depend on configured emission factors.
* ESG scoring is an internal assessment methodology and is not an official certification.
* AI-generated insights may require human review.
* Compliance tracking does not constitute legal advice.
* Document extraction may depend on document structure and quality.
* Production deployment would require additional security, scalability, monitoring, and compliance controls.
